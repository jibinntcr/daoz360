## 3. Creative direction

Concept name for the design team: Everything comes together. Do not print this instruction as a product feature.

Visual story: separate content objects gently assemble around one central orange brand element. The objects then settle into an orderly calendar. This communicates organisation, coordination and consistency. Avoid a disconnected collection of floating social logos.

Orange from the supplied logo is the primary brand colour across the website. Give it a consistent, prominent role in the hero, primary actions, active states and 3D artwork. As a rough composition guide, use 65% white/light neutral surfaces, 20% orange and pale orange surfaces, and 15% charcoal text or panels. These proportions are a design guide rather than a rigid pixel target. Keep white space generous so the orange remains distinctive. One charcoal closing section with orange accents creates a clear final conversion point.

### Brand application rules

Brand name: DAOZ360 in editorial text. Use the supplied lowercase custom wordmark in artwork. Never substitute DAOZ AI. The original domain remains https://daoz360.ai/; the .ai domain extension is not part of the wordmark.

Logo: use the supplied full horizontal wordmark for the website header, with its orange 360 on the same baseline. Use the orange emblem alone for favicon, compact app icons and social profile pictures. The full stacked emblem/wordmark/tagline artwork is suitable for larger brand placements; avoid squeezing the tagline into a small navigation bar.

Clear space: as a proposed rule, reserve at least one-quarter of the emblem width around the emblem, or one-quarter of the wordmark letter height around a standalone wordmark. Starting minimums: emblem 32 px high; wordmark 140 px wide. Check at actual display size; omit the tagline from compact lockups and use the supplied emblem instead if legibility suffers.

Preserve aspect ratio, lettering, chat bubble, three dots, lightning detail and logo gradient. Do not stretch, rotate, crop the mark, rearrange the digits or regenerate it. Use white/light neutral backgrounds for the supplied orange-and-black full logo. On dark sections, use an approved reversed wordmark if available; otherwise place the original in a clean white container. Do not invent an unapproved colour variant.

Gradient: golden-orange to vivid-orange is a supporting brand expression for the emblem and 3D illustration materials. Keep UI labels and body copy flat and high contrast. Do not apply the gradient to every heading, card or button.

Type: Manrope headings and Inter body are proposed website typography, not a replacement for the custom logo lettering. Use sentence case, clear hierarchy and restrained weights. Avoid all-caps paragraphs.

Voice: clear, capable, approachable and practical. Explain the work users can accomplish. Use short active sentences. Keep the established line Plan. Publish. Grow. for marketing; retain MANAGE. CONNECT. GROW when using the supplied tagline artwork. Do not combine both taglines into a new logo lockup. Avoid unsupported superlatives or guaranteed growth promises.

Components: orange is the consistent primary-action colour; neutral secondary buttons have dark text and a subtle border. Use deep orange for hover/pressed states and a clearly visible dark focus outline with separation from the fill. Active tabs combine colour with an underline or shape. Do not use orange as the sole indicator of an error or success; include readable labels and icons.

3D style: warm-orange hero objects, warm-white ceramic supporting objects, soft shadows, consistent lighting and sparse compositions. Preserve the exact flat logo as an independent asset. Keep decorative motion separate from the brand master. Match every feature illustration to this material and lighting system.

Do not introduce purple/blue neon gradients, unrelated accent palettes, multicoloured feature cards or stock cartoon characters. Official third-party platform colours may appear only when their supported integration is verified; they do not change DAOZ360's own palette.

Typography: Manrope for headings, Inter for body. Self-host font files when practical. Hero heading 64–76 px desktop, 38–44 px mobile. Section headings 40–48 px desktop, 28–34 px mobile. Body 17–18 px. Buttons 15–16 px semibold. Use comfortable line lengths, with text paragraphs around 55–70 characters wide.

Layout: maximum content width 1200 px; desktop side gutters at least 32 px; mobile 20 px. Section spacing around 96–112 px desktop and 56–72 px mobile. Cards 20–24 px radius, subtle border and soft shadow. Avoid giving every section identical cards. Use split layouts, one deliberate feature grid, workflow tabs and a strong closing panel.

## 7. 3D assets and animation specification

### Hero concept — the connected workspace

One orange sculptural chat form inspired by the brand, with three or four small white ceramic objects: a calendar tile, content card, image tile and approval check. Objects should feel like physical desktop pieces. Use pale warm shadows, a white background, soft studio light and restrained material contrast. Keep the exact supplied flat logo separately in the navigation; a decorative 3D object is not a replacement brand master.

Hero layout: left 48% copy; right 52% visual on large screens. On mobile: copy and buttons first, then a smaller visual. Keep text out of the render; all headings, labels and buttons are real HTML.

Motion: one gentle assembly entrance, then a static resting state. Add an optional Play animation control for replay. Avoid perpetual orbiting, constantly turning logos, cursor-following cameras or moving text.

A realistic live 3D scene is optional. For the first version, use high-quality pre-rendered transparent assets arranged in layers, animated with subtle CSS transforms. This can create depth without requiring a full-page 3D engine. If actual WebGL is used, load it after the static hero is visible and provide a poster fallback.

Each successful delivery to one account counts once. Publishing the same post to three accounts uses three deliveries. Drafts and failed attempts do not consume the allowance.

Are these prices per user?
Each plan includes the stated number of user seats. Your subscription price covers that bundle; it is not multiplied by every included user.

How does yearly billing work?
Pay once for twelve months of access at the price of ten monthly payments. The page shows both the monthly equivalent and the amount charged yearly.

Does the annual plan include a full year's publishing allowance immediately?
No. Publishing capacity renews each subscription month, including on yearly plans.

Are taxes included?
Displayed prices exclude applicable taxes. Review the final total before completing payment.

What happens if I need a larger allowance?
Choose a larger plan or contact us about your requirements. We will show any price change before you agree to it.

Does this include social media management services?
These are software subscriptions. Content production, campaign execution, ad spend and done-for-you social media services require a separate agreement.

Can I change my plan?
Contact us or use the available billing controls. Any effective date, price difference or proration must be shown before you confirm a change.

Can I cancel?
Under the proposed billing model, you can turn off renewal and retain access until your paid period ends. Display this answer publicly only once the actual cancellation flow and approved policy support it.

Which networks can I use?
Use the supported-platform list for the current product. Account limits describe how many profiles you can connect, not a promise that every network or format is supported.
